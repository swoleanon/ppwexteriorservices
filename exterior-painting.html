Paint that lasts because the surface was prepared.

Florida sun, salt, and mildew punish exterior coatings. PPW washes first—pressure or soft wash, depending on the material—then paints body, trim, doors, fascia, and stamped concrete so the finish is applied to a clean, dry surface.

Wash, Then Paint

A new coat over mildew is a short-lived job.

We treat painting as the last step of an exterior refresh, not a cover-up. Organic staining, chalking, and dirt are removed first. Then we prime where needed and apply coatings rated for South Florida exposure. Scope is written before work starts: which elevations, how many coats, and whether trim, doors, or decorative concrete are included.

What We Paint

Buildings and the hardscapes attached to them.

Painting can be a full elevation refresh or a targeted repair after a wash. We quote both.

See Finished Surfaces
01

Residential Exteriors

Whole-house or single-elevation painting. Color changes, faded stucco, and patchy previous coats are common requests after a house wash.

02

Trim, Doors & Fascia

The details that make a clean house look finished. Often painted on a different cycle than the body.

03

Stamped & Decorative Concrete

Worn coatings on stamped driveways and walkways. We clean, then recast the color so the pattern reads again instead of looking gray and tired.

04

Commercial Buildings

Offices, retail, and managed properties that need a professional exterior without shutting the site down longer than necessary.

05

Prep & Spot Repair

Scraping failed film, caulking, and priming bare areas so the new coat does not fail at the same spots.

06

Coordinated Exterior Projects

Wash, paint, and glass in one plan so crews are not painting around dirty walks or streaked windows.
