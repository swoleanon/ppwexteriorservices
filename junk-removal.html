Items that require separate handling

We do not accept hazardous chemicals, fuel, wet paint, asbestos, biohazard material, explosives, or other regulated waste. Refrigerant-containing appliances, electronics, tires, batteries, and especially heavy material may require advance approval, additional handling, or a different disposal provider.
