Commercial exterior care in Broward, Miami-Dade & Palm Beach

Preferred Power Washing provides pressure washing, soft washing, window cleaning, exterior painting, landscaping, and recurring property maintenance for restaurants, retail and storefronts, offices, HOAs and condominiums, multifamily communities, and multi-site clients across Broward, Miami-Dade, and Palm Beach Counties.

Commercial service is scoped to minimize disruption while keeping entries, sidewalks, glass, customer-facing areas, and property frontage presentable. Window care can run weekly, biweekly, monthly, or custom. Pressure and soft washing may be monthly, quarterly, or project-based. Landscape care is typically the weekly piece. Exterior painting is quoted for storefronts, offices, and common buildings when a finish refresh is due.

Property managers get defined scopes and recurring schedules instead of juggling a different vendor for every surface. Request a commercial proposal.
