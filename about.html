The Story

From first equipment to a full exterior company.

In 2019, while attending college and studying EMT and firefighting, Jordan founded PPW Exterior Services. After working alongside experienced leaders in the industry, he had a clear picture of what high-quality service required: the right equipment, attention to detail, strong communication, and a commitment to doing the job correctly.

With limited resources and a clear plan, he saved enough to purchase his first professional equipment and began servicing residential driveways and patios throughout South Florida.
