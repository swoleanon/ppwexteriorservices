The Story

From first equipment to a full exterior company.

PPW did not start as a large crew with a full shop. It started with a clear standard and the decision to do the work correctly.

In 2019, while attending college and studying EMT and firefighting, Jordan founded Preferred Power Washing. After working alongside experienced leaders in the industry, he had a clear picture of what high-quality service required: the right equipment, attention to detail, strong communication, and a commitment to doing the job correctly.

With limited resources and a clear plan, he saved enough to purchase his first professional equipment and began servicing residential driveways and patios throughout South Florida. The company grew as he reinvested in better gear, expanded what the crew could take on, and kept building experience. Roof and exterior soft washing followed. Preferred Power Washing has since become a multi-service exterior property maintenance company for both residential and commercial clients.

Today, PPW provides pressure cleaning, soft washing, painting, paver sealing, window cleaning, and lawn maintenance throughout Broward, Miami-Dade, and Palm Beach Counties, and travels for larger commercial projects. Jordan and his professional crews have completed work for HOAs, shopping centers, restaurants, hotels, office buildings, apartment communities, parking garages, luxury homes, and other commercial properties across South Florida.
